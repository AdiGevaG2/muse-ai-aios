# Adult and AI merchants

Adult (5967) and dating (7273) share most of their risk surface. Generative AI
is now reshaping both — as a new violation vector and as a moderation tool.

## MCC 5967 — direct marketing: inbound teleservices

Risks: content violations; closed member areas; user- and live-generated
content; ads; TC40 and disputes; trials and subscriptions; discrete billing with
unclear descriptors; aggregation support websites.

Mitigation: initial content screening **including gated areas** (with login
credentials); verify the platform has a policy letting depicted persons appeal
for content removal; KYC on content creators and models; review content prior to
publication; review backlink structure.

Traffic: mainly direct (repeat customers) and search. Low ad traffic is expected
since most advertising platforms restrict sexually explicit content — so
referral traffic warrants scrutiny. [documented — Berlin *Adult and AI*]

Note this aligns with the MMP requirement to monitor members-only areas — see
[[mastercard-bram-and-mmp]].

## MCC 7273 — dating and escort services

Same base risks, plus: chat bots and "moderators"; mirror websites and load
balancing; the blurred line to escort services.

Mitigation adds: confirm the merchant does not use deceptive tactics such as
chat bots or fake profiles; track the customer satisfaction record closely.

## Card scheme rules

Written provider agreements: merchants must contractually prohibit illegal
activity and require documented consent and age/identity verification for every
person depicted. Requirements cover verified upload controls, pre-publication
review, live content control, responsible marketing and complaints handling,
consent appeals and reporting, and anti-trafficking with audit access.
[documented]

Mastercard Rule 5.12.7, as revised 24 February 2026, prohibits sale of any
product, service or image — **including AI-generated or digitally altered
content** — that is patently offensive and lacks serious artistic value:
non-consensual sexual behaviour, sexual exploitation of minors, non-consensual
mutilation, bestiality, and **unauthorised synthetic depictions of a real
person's likeness**. See [[mastercard-bram-and-mmp]].

## How AI changes 5967 and 7273

Risks: AI-generated profiles on dating sites; highly effective agents that chat
to entice consumers while tracking their behaviour; ability to generate explicit
imagery, or imagery violating copyright or the right to one's likeness.

Opportunity: more effective and scalable content moderation tools for 5967/7273
platforms. [documented]

The same capability sits on both sides — generation and moderation — so the
underwriting question shifts from "does the platform use AI" to "whose AI is
winning on this platform". [inference]
