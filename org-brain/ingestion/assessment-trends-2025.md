## Reading it

Gambling dominates assessments (42%) just as it dominates TL beneficiary
analysis (51% of incident reports 2020–2025). The two datasets are different
lenses on the same concentration. See [[gambling-and-miscoding]],
[[transaction-laundering]].

Pharmaceuticals at 15% plus tainted nutraceuticals at 8% make the
ingestibles cluster the clear number two — consistent with Mastercard's 2026
focus on RUO peptides. See [[peptide-merchants]].

Long-tail categories each sit at 1–4% but carry disproportionate severity: CSAM
at 1% is not a small problem, it is a small *count*. Assessment share is a poor
proxy for harm. [inference]
