# Competitive Landscape — G2RS

Org-wide reference. Human-owned. Provenance-tagged. Source: internal
business-context brief, 2026-06-22.

## Current competitors (post-consolidation)
[documented-public] G2RS competes in the MMSP and marketplace-risk spaces against:
- LegitScript — MMSP, merchant certification, content compliance; strong in
  high-risk verticals (pharmacy, telemedicine, gambling); larger analyst team.
- Ballerine — open-source identity/risk platform; merchant onboarding + KYB;
  startup-focused.
- Coris — AI-powered merchant risk and compliance platform.
- TrueBiz — business verification / merchant monitoring and due diligence; a
  Mastercard MMP-approved provider.
- Austreme — merchant compliance monitoring; registered Mastercard MMSP and Visa
  TPA.
- OnLayer (formerly PCI Checklist) — merchant risk / onboarding / compliance
  monitoring.

[documented] Large marketplaces (Amazon, eBay) run significant in-house product
moderation — an implicit "build vs. buy" alternative for platform operators.

## Former competitors — now consolidated under G2RS
- [documented-public] WebShield — was a merchant-monitoring/compliance competitor;
  now under G2RS ownership. Note: G2RS acquired WebShield's parent company, ZignSec
  AB, which brought WebShield under G2RS — directionally "now owned by G2RS," but
  via the parent-company deal, not a direct WebShield acquisition. No longer a
  competitor.
- [documented-public] EverC — was a marketplace-risk/merchant-monitoring
  competitor; acquisition finalized Oct 6, 2025; now part of G2RS. No longer a
  competitor.

## Consolidation timeline (important for framing)
- [documented] Before Oct 2025: EverC vs. G2RS vs. WebShield were three separate
  competitors.
- [documented] After Oct 2025: all one company under G2RS ownership.

## G2RS advantages
- [internal-brief] Merchant Map® data asset (20+ yrs, "75%+ of global acquirers")
  — proprietary/internal metric, not publicly verifiable; confirm against internal
  source.
- [internal-brief] Mastercard-registered MMSP status — confirm against internal
  registration record (WebShield lineage is public; G2RS's own registration is
  internal).
- [documented-public] Consolidated offering: merchant monitoring + marketplace
  risk + fraud detection in one platform vs. point solutions.
- [internal-brief] Network effect across "300+ partner FIs" — proprietary count,
  not publicly verifiable.

## Positioning note
[documented] G2RS is a compliance-first MMSP, not a generic "fraud prevention"
tool. Primary value is helping FIs meet Visa/Mastercard requirements.
