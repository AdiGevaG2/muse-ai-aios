### Slide 18 — Data quality use cases

- **Credit bureau (Top 3)** — Previous vendor: 7.54% of bankruptcy records had an
  incorrect bankruptcy status. Consequences: inaccuracies led to losing clients,
  brand damage, and threat of lawsuits. G2RS results: bankruptcy automation
  improved recoveries, brand image, and competitiveness.
- **Mortgage servicer (Top 5)** — Previous vendor: out of 16,000 bankruptcies,
  2,200 were false positives and 2,800 were missed. Consequences: errors led to
  lost recoveries, compliance issues, and the need to manually update system of
  record with correct disposition information. G2RS results: bankruptcy
  monitoring increased recoveries and efficiency while decreasing costs.
- **Debt buyer (Top 5)** — Previous vendor failed to notify the client of over
  443,000 bankruptcies and 46,000 deceased hits. Consequences: unidentified
  bankruptcies led to missed Bar Dates, decreased recoveries, and lawsuits due to
  automatic stay violations. G2RS results: increased revenue, reduced costs, and
  significant efficiency gains.
- **Enterprise bank (Top 3)** — Previous vendor: limited docket event categories
  (~70) left a significant amount of "noise" in output files. Consequences:
  insufficient docket event categorization led to significant ongoing FTE costs
  to manually review docket events for relevance. G2RS results: docket event
  identification filtered out nearly 90% of irrelevant [events], dramatically
  reducing load on FTEs.
