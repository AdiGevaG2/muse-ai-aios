## What EverC was / does
- [documented] AI-first marketplace risk and merchant-fraud detection specialist.
- [documented] Marketplace monitoring: identify/remove counterfeit, hazardous,
  illegal, and recalled products via multi-modal AI (text + image + metadata).
- [documented] Seller fraud detection: risk scoring, fraud-pattern detection,
  risk-graph mapping (connections between sellers, domains, payment accounts),
  reputation tracking.
- [documented] Merchant monitoring & portfolio risk: fraud indicators, compliance
  violations, behavioral/transaction anomalies.

## Scale & accuracy claims
- [documented] "30M+ items scanned daily"; ~95% precision (per Wish case study).
- [documented] Some product-risk detection/removal workflows are described in
  marketing/case-study material as operating in minutes/seconds (Wish: "in as
  quickly as seconds").
- [hunch] Treat marketing scale/accuracy/speed figures as vendor/client

  this is a relative-strength judgment, not an absence.
