**The Problem**

Merchant risk cannot be fully understood at onboarding alone.

Hidden connections and changing merchant behavior introduce exposure over time,
making it difficult to maintain consistent controls and expand safely.

**The Solution – MerchantView**

MerchantView is an ongoing monitoring solution that evaluates risk across the
full merchant lifecycle.

It combines proprietary data, AI-driven insights, and network intelligence to
assess merchants and maintain visibility as merchant behavior and risk evolve
over time.

**Key Outcomes**

- Maintain continuous visibility into evolving merchant risk
- Identify hidden connections and emerging risk as behavior changes
- Support safe expansion into new regions and verticals
- Reduce reactive enforcement by surfacing risk earlier

**Why MerchantView**

Because merchant risk is driven by evolving behavior and connected activity, not
point-in-time signals.

MerchantView — Continuous visibility into merchant risk across the full
lifecycle. AI-driven merchant risk intelligence, delivered in minutes.
