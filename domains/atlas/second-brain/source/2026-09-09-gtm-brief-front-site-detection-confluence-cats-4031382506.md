### **What the Solution Does (Value in 30 Seconds)**

**FSD detects businesses that look polished on the outside but don’t behave like real businesses anywhere else.**

**What problem FSD solves**  
FSD identifies merchants that appear legitimate at first glance but are likely *constructed fronts* - entities designed to pass basic onboarding checks while hiding higher-risk activity elsewhere.

This signal is designed to surface **early detection of high-confidence risks** that warrant immediate attention, before financial exposure or regulatory damage occurs.

**How (Super short, elaborated below)?**

Using various data points, harnessing expert insights, Generative AI & ML to evaluate whether a site acts, behaves, and *exists* like a legitimate business - or whether it’s a constructed front designed for fraud, scams, laundering, or bypassing onboarding controls.

### **Why Customers Care (Short)**

**Front-site risk is costly, hard to detect, and easy to miss until it’s too late. **Traditional onboarding and monitoring checks focus on what a merchant *claims*. Front sites are built to pass those checks while hiding risk elsewhere.

- Detects **fronts, fake merchants, and laundering networks** that traditional tools miss, upfront, **preventing unexpected loss incurred by sudden chargeback surges, or regulatory fines.**
- Reduces **manual review effort (*****a high-volume,***​ ***low-margin***).
- Reduces **risk of onboarding bad actors.**
- Increases **approval rates of good merchants** (fewer false positives).
- Creates **explainable, stable risk scoring** for onboarding and monitoring.

### **How FSD is meant to be used**

- In this phase **we are rolling out a binary output - True or False, **utilizing only our “critical” grade findings** (\>90% precision) **(In the future, we would like to roll out also, e.g., “suspected FSD”)
- FSD is a **call-to-action**, a **critical-risk escalation signal**, not as informational noise.
- An FSD finding means:  
**“This merchant requires immediate review before activation or continuation.”**
- The expected action is **manual review or investigation**, guided by the surfaced evidence.
- Knowledge is working to create a **user manual**, that our clients and support will to get.

FSD is not designed to auto-block merchants on its own, but to ensure that **the riskiest cases are never missed or delayed**.

**The Future of FSD - Where we are headed**

We plan on developing this top of funnel capability into a higher resolution solution, where FSD is surfaced with more signals, and with the ability to differ between different risks, e.g., FSD\_scams, FSD\_TL (Q4-26-Q1-27). 

In our roadmap, we plan on repurposing these signals, detection and analysis as part of a future Scams full flow & solution (***2027***).

### **Elaborate solution, Core Signals Used in the Model**

#### **Behavioral Signals – *****What the site does***

**One-liner:**  
“We check whether the website looks real - unique content, a consistent story, and pricing that makes sense. Such signals reflect a transition from expert investigations that historically required manual collection, to a process in which they are collected, evaluated, and utilized at scale”

**Breakdown:**

- **Content Uniqueness** → detects templated, autogenerated, vague, or generic content.
- **Content Consistency** → the business story must match across the domain, homepage, SERP, and legal pages.
- **Pricing vs. Offering** → unrealistic prices, vertical–pricing mismatch, or in-site inconsistencies.

#### **Domain Integrity – *****How the domain behaves***

**One-liner:**  
“A real business invests in its domain; front sites behave like disposable assets.”

**Breakdown:**

- Domain age, update patterns, registrar metadata
- Short expiration horizon → disposable
- Very new domain → high risk
- Cheap/Scam-pattern TLDs (.xyz, .top, random strings)

**Example meta block:**

```json
{
 "date_created": "2016-06-22",
 "date_expires": "2026-06-22",
 "date_updated": "2025-11-25"
}
```

#### **Online Presence – *****Does this business exist anywhere else?***

**One-liner:**  
“Legitimate businesses leave a digital footprint; front sites don’t.”

**Breakdown:**

- Reviews, directories, press
- Social profiles & engagement
- Mentions across the ecosystem
- Alignment between claims (e.g., “10 years in business”) and real-world evidence

**Example:**  
A “10-year SaaS leader” with zero social presence and zero external mentions → strong contradiction → risk signal.

### **How We Use These Signals**

**One-liner:**

“Built on expert-designed signal frameworks, leveraging Generative AI to extract subtle risk signals at scale. These signals - together with domain metadata and online presence - are evaluated by ML models to produce a clear, reliable risk decision - that scales.”  
More elaborate:

”Built on **expert-designed signal frameworks**, leveraging **Generative AI** to extract subtle risk signals at a large-scale, turning detecting FSDs into **a scalable, big-data enabled solution**; 

Our engine then **weighs in** the extracted signals, with additional **diverse site signals** (URL’s structure, domain metadata, online presence)​, evaluated by ML models to produce **a clear, reliable risk decision**.”

#### **Flow Breakdown**

- LLM extracts high-context features from site behavior, from SERP inputs.
- Metadata and other information is collected. 
- Data is used → Features are calculated.
- Structured features → **ML Decision model**
- Model weighs interactions between signals, e.g., consider:
    - Uniqueness ✕ Domain quality ✕ Online footprint ✕ Pricing realism X Transactional flow X Policy adherence
- Calculates the probability of being a legitimate site vs. a front site** → Then, a threshold is set per use case type/ portfolio type, to provide the client with a binary response** (only Critical ones for this point in time).

#### **Providing high clarity Evidence & explainability**  

Every FSD finding is accompanied by **clear, human-readable evidence**, including:

- Behavioral inconsistencies
- Domain integrity signals
- Online presence contradictions
- **Examples**:

|  |
| --- |
| This tangible goods merchant has an isolated digital footprint and limited visibility across recognized validation channels. It further shows sub-par operational history and inconsistent policy compliance, confirming the entity's unsuitability for continued merchant services. |
| This tangible goods merchant exhibits an isolated digital footprint and a limited visibility across recognized validation channels, while having sub-par operational history, confirming the entity's unsuitability for continued merchant services. |
| This digital goods merchant has an isolated digital presence across validation channels, shows sub par operational history, and operates a suspicious rasnactional flow, confirming its high-risk profile. |

### **What Makes Our Approach Unique (“Secret Sauce”)**

#### **The solution architecture - solving a human-level fraud problem by combining human reasoning with scalable AI**

Front sites are built explicitly to bypass rule-based checks, not disclosing overt signals.

Our hybrid solution blends:

- **Human-derived logic** (behavioural cues, inconsistencies, footprint reasoning), regularly checked and agilely updated (**Human-in-the-loop adaptiveness** for new threat patterns).
- **LLM reasoning at scale**
- **Domain data and Metadata.**
- **ML classification for signal weighting**

This creates a model that differs between good and bad sites, while it continuously adapts to the evolving fraud landscape.

#### **We Have Unique Data → Built on Proprietary Data**

Proprietary, unified merchant datasets across G2RS X EC, give us a foundation no competitor has - enabling the development of new risk capabilities like FSD, with higher confidence and broader coverage from day one.

### **Why Customers Care (Long)**

Front-site risk is costly, hard to detect, and easy to miss until it’s too late. Traditional onboarding and monitoring checks focus on what a merchant claims. Front sites are built to pass those checks while hiding risk elsewhere.

**FSD changes that.**

- **Catches what other controls miss**  
Identifies merchants that look legitimate on the surface but behave unlike real businesses across the wider ecosystem.
- **Prevents worst-case outcomes early**  
Surfaces high-confidence risk before activation or deep exposure, reducing the chance of sudden chargebacks, regulatory action, or reputational damage.
- **Reduces manual effort where it matters most**  
Flags a small, controlled set of merchants that genuinely require human review, instead of flooding teams with low-value noise.
- **Improves decision confidence**
    - Provides clear, explainable reasoning so risk and compliance teams understand why a merchant was flagged - not just that it was.
    - Acts as a high-confidence power signal that strengthens existing onboarding and monitoring decisions when combined with other risk indicators.
- **Protects good merchants while stopping bad actors**  
Fewer false positives mean faster approvals for legitimate businesses, without compromising risk standards.
