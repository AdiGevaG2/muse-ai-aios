## Meeting summary

1. Meeting Summary
The meeting focused on presenting updates, new features, and advanced capabilities in the Atlas system (as a replacement/upgrade to the existing system), with a focus on improving risk management processes, ecosystem site tracking (Connected Sites), AI-driven automation, and reducing false positives.

Key Points Discussed:
Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR): The company will begin holding regular QBRs to showcase product development and new features.
Ecosystem Management (Connected Sites): The system identifies URLs and related links (including merchant category codes and mismatches compared to reported data).
Noise and False Positive Reduction: Upgrading models to reduce unnecessary alerts (shifting from an average of 1-to-4 to 1-to-a-quarter for connections), presenting only strong and accurate connections (such as strict IP matches).
Planned Features for the Upcoming Quarter and February 2027:
Front-side detection, scam detection, web traffic analysis, watchlists, and sanctions.
Deep expansion of website policy checks.
Use of Web ID and the ability to upload files without URLs for automated scanning.
AI-powered Identity Underwriting to shorten underwriting processes and enable faster merchant onboarding.
A new Report Center, detailed Activity Log, and evidence screenshots for all findings.
Client Migration: A structured plan for migrating all client data from Merchant View into Atlas without losing historical information.
2. Action Items
For the Development/Implementation Team (Service Providers):
Organize a targeted training session and a full system demo of Atlas for the client (including the new Merchant-addition Wizard and workflows).
Provide a customized demo environment with client data for hands-on testing and evaluation.

For the Client Side:
