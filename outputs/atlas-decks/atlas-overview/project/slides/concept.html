The concept

Four Ideas Behind Atlas

One record, start to finish

A merchant keeps one record from onboarding through monitoring. Its history follows it.

Every check on one screen

MCC, content, sanctions, reviews, and connected sites, side by side, each with its evidence.

AI that reads the whole website

Text, images, and metadata are scanned together, the way an analyst reads a site.

Set once per portfolio

You set services and schedules on the portfolio. Every merchant added to it runs them.

Confidential and Proprietary – Do Not Distribute | 6

A portfolio is the group of merchants a client manages under one setup. Setting services there means analysts never configure merchant by merchant.
