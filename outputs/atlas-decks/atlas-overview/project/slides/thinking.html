Three Rules Behind Every Atlas Decision

01

Read the code, not the brochure

We trace what each product does in its code, then compare it to Atlas, capability by capability.

02

One answer per overlap

Where two products do the same job differently, we decide how Atlas behaves. We don’t stack both.

03

Prove parity before the move

You move only after Atlas is validated against what you use today.
