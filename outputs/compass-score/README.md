# Compass Score

**Stage:** Knowledge capture (pre-discovery)
**Status:** Raw requirement notes being gathered from PM. No discovery/PRD started.

## What it is

Compass Score® aggregates merchant risk findings across components (e.g. MCC,
Merchant Risk History/NRH, Sanctions/Legal, Content Compliance, Operational
Risk, Consumer Ratings & Adverse Media) into one merchant risk summary, so
underwriters can see overall risk and drill into every signal.

## Artifacts

- [Knowledge notes](knowledge/compass-score-knowledge.md) — raw requirement
  notes captured from the PM, not yet structured into discovery/PRD.

## Open decisions

- Display format: percentage vs. 0-1000 numeric score (see knowledge doc).
- Component weighting model: per-portfolio client-configured percentages,
  mandatory aggregation to 100%; dashboard (multi-portfolio) view needs its
  own mandated weighting across all components present in the org.
