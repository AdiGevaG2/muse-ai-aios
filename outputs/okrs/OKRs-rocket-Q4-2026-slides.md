# Rocket Team OKRs, Q4 2026: Slide Content

One slide per company Key Result. Each Rocket project appears once, under the Key Result it moves most. Projects: items marked Must in the Q4 group of the Atlas Migration Packages Roadmap monday board, Dev Team = Rocket.

NAB parity success metrics are measured in Q4. Success metrics for other projects are measured from each project's release. Secondary success metrics are measured 30 days after NAB migrates to Atlas on slides 1 and 2, and 30 days after release on slides 3 and 4. Slide 5 success metrics are measured monthly in Q4. Success metric format: metric, baseline to target.

Terms: NAB is the first client to migrate to Atlas. NAB parity projects are the projects marked NAB on the monday board; they are ready and used by NAB by December 31, 2026. MCC is the Merchant Category Code. WSL is the Watchlist, Sanctions, and Legal check. CRAM is the Consumer Ratings and Adverse Media check. POC is a proof of concept.
