## Slide 1

**Objective:** Bottom Line Efficiencies
**Product OKR:** Analyst efficiency and faster reporting
**Project Name:** Full MCC Parity, MVP
**What:** Atlas checks whether a merchant's website matches its category code, at parity with Single Platform.
**Why:** Clients get mismatches without waiting for a G2RS analyst, and analysts spend their time on cases that need judgment.
**Success criteria:** Share of MCC assessments completed with no analyst action.

**Objective:** Top Line Sustainable Growth
**Product OKR:** New ARR Enabled by 2026 Product Investments (Net New and Cross Sell): MCC
**Project Name:** Merchant Category Code (MCC) Enhancements
