# Adyen Product Feedback Session, 2026-06-11

Sasha Kononov walked G2RS through feedback on the full URL monitoring lifecycle in MerchantView, as input for Atlas. G2RS attendees: Mary Williams, Ishai Froind, Sarah Craven, Juan Jose Jovel Ramos, Gal Sahar. Source: internal notes, pasted by Adi Geva on 2026-11-13.

## How Adyen works

- Adyen uploads URLs by hand. API integration waits on a possible change to Adyen's internal case management system.
- The portfolio holds about 100 platforms. A platform is a legal entity with many sub-merchants, called account holders.
- Adyen assigns each analyst to platforms for the full cycle: review, then merchant outreach.
- Adyen's false positive rate was 8-10% excluding offline findings, against a 5% target.

The common thread: **Adyen thinks in platforms, and MerchantView only knows portfolios, merchants and URLs.** Eight of the twelve themes below come back to that gap.

## Atlas gaps that hit Adyen's daily work

The parity check lists MerchantView capabilities Atlas lacks. Two of them sit at the center of how Adyen's team works today:

| Gap in Atlas | Adyen's use in MerchantView, 90 days |
|---|---|
| Text snippet and keyword evidence | 1,288 evidence reviews by 9 users |
| Finding change history | 12,982 finding logs opened by 10 users |

Notes on a finding are not a gap. Atlas has comments per finding and per merchant (Adi Geva, 2026-11-13); the parity check's "notes per merchant and per dispute only" is out of date. Adyen added 9,972 finding notes in 90 days, so this carries over.

Other parity gaps that touch Adyen: no client-triggered rescan of a single URL, no PDF report (Adyen generated 42 reports in 90 days), no separate severity for reported and unreported URLs.

## Related QBR ask

Adyen's "website change detection" request on the QBR deck matches the Change Detection PRD (draft, 2026-10-20). Its MVP replaces the Visualping vendor with G2RS's own capability, delivered as the Excel report clients receive today. Atlas integration comes in a later phase.

## G2RS follow-ups agreed in the session

- Evaluate each item for Atlas monitoring requirements after the first release.
- Assess whether a platform flag or sub-object can carry scoped risk matrix rules.
- Define dispute flow requirements.
- Evaluate duplicate-URL handling within merchant management.
- Share a sorted roadmap at the 2026-07-02 follow-up: what MerchantView can do now, what is Atlas, what is out of scope. The outcome of that meeting is not on record.
