# Atlas Training Plan for Customer Success and Sales

Owner: Adi Geva, Senior PM, Atlas
Status: Draft, 2026-11-16

## Goal

Customer Success Managers and Sales can explain Atlas, its roadmap, and the move from the legacy products to clients, without product team support on the call.

- **Why we need it:** Atlas replaces MerchantView, WebShield, and Single Platform, and every client moves to it. Customer Success and Sales lead those client conversations.
- **Value for the client:** clients get one consistent answer on what Atlas does, what is coming, and what changes for them.
- **Why now:** the quarter runs November 2026 to February 2027, and NAB is due at full parity in production on December 31, 2026. Client conversations about the move start before that date.

## Audience

- Customer Success Managers
- Sales

Sessions 1 and 2 are joint. Session 3 is for Sales and session 4 is for Customer Success.

## Sessions

Four live sessions, 1 hour each. Content comes from the Atlas Overview deck. No new deck is built for this plan.

### Session 1: What Atlas is

| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | Customer Success and Sales together |
| Goal | Attendees can explain why Atlas exists, what it replaces, and how a client uses it |
| Content | Atlas Overview deck, sections 1 to 4: who G2RS is, the problem, the idea behind Atlas, the product step by step, how we think and build |
| Live demo | Add a merchant, monitoring, findings, and merchant intelligence in Atlas |
| Attendees leave able to | Run the client walkthrough from the deck on their own |

### Session 2: The roadmap

| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | Customer Success and Sales together |
| Goal | Attendees can present this quarter's work and the 2027 direction, and know what not to promise |
| Content | Atlas Overview deck, section 5: this quarter's four outcomes and where Atlas goes in 2027 |
| This quarter | Faster analyst decisions, one platform, Graph and Merchant Map®, focus on what needs a decision |
| 2027 direction | Scams solution, Compass Score®, change detection in Atlas |
| Attendees leave able to | Present the roadmap slides and answer timing questions within the rules below |

### Session 3: Sales

| | |
|---|---|
| Goal | Sales can position Atlas to new and current clients |
| Content | Positioning against the legacy products, answers to common objections, what is on the roadmap and what is not |
| Exercise | Role play: a client asks what they gain by moving to Atlas and when a feature arrives |

### Session 4: Customer Success

| | |
|---|---|
| Goal | Customer Success can guide a client through the move to Atlas |
| Content | Atlas Overview deck, section 6, internal only: migration plan, NAB first, open risks. Client questions about the move and the answers to give |
| Exercise | Role play: a MerchantView client asks what changes for them and when they move |

Sessions 3 and 4 each end with a knowledge check.

## What to Tell Clients

- Present roadmap themes without dates.
- 2027 items are direction, not committed dates.
- MerchantView, WebShield, and Single Platform keep running for each client until that client moves to Atlas.
- Do not share a date for retiring the legacy products. No date is committed.
- Remove the section 6 slides before any client presentation.
- External use of the deck needs Marketing approval.

## How We Know It Worked

Each attendee passes the knowledge check at the end of their role session, session 3 for Sales and session 4 for Customer Success.

## Logistics

- Presenter: Adi Geva presents all four sessions.
- Length: 1 hour per session.
- Recording: each session is recorded and shared with attendees and new hires.
